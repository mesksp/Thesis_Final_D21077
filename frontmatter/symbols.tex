Symbols used consistently for a single quantity throughout the thesis are listed once. A small
number of symbols are, by established convention in the sub-field each chapter draws on, used for
more than one quantity; these are listed once per meaning, tagged with the chapter(s) or appendix in
which that meaning applies.

$\mathbf{v}$ & Cartesian linear velocity / twist command & Ch.~4, 6, 7 \\
$\mathbf{e}$ & Pose-error vector, position ($\mathbf{e}_p$) or orientation ($\mathbf{e}_o$); in Ch.~6 the goal-attraction error $\mathbf{g}-\mathbf{p}_{ee}^\alpha$ & Ch.~4, 6, 8 \\
$\mathbf{f}$ & iAPF force vector ($\mathbf{f}_{\text{att}}, \mathbf{f}_{\text{rep}}, \mathbf{f}_{\text{home}}, \mathbf{f}_{\text{damp}}$) & Ch.~6, 7 \\

$d$ & Scalar Euclidean distance between two points, segments, or links & Ch.~5, 6, 7; App.~B \\
$D$ & Chamfer-matching cost between a template and the scene & Ch.~3 \\
$D$ & Inter-arm / inter-link distance matrix & Ch.~5, 6 \\
$k$ & Generic index (loop, joint, or summation counter) & throughout \\
$k_a, k_r, k_h, k_d, k_v$ & iAPF gain constants: attraction, repulsion, home-seeking, damping, velocity scaling & Ch.~6 \\
$L$ & A line segment, $L_1, L_2$ & Ch.~5; App.~B \\
$V$ & RL state-value function, $V_\theta(\mathbf{s})$ & Ch.~8; App.~D \\
$N$ & A count: template points (Ch.~3), arms (Ch.~5), or rollout steps $N_{\text{steps}}$ (Ch.~8) & Ch.~3, 5, 8 \\
$\hat{A}_t$ & Generalized Advantage Estimation advantage estimate & Ch.~8; App.~D \\
$S$ & Centralized-critic global state (prior guided policy) & Ch.~8 \\
$\mathbf{s}$ & Local agent observation/state & Ch.~8; App.~D \\
$\mathbf{a}$ & Policy action (Ch.~8); in Ch.~5, the start point of a line segment & Ch.~5, 8 \\
$r$ & Reward & Ch.~8; App.~D \\
$\mathbf{w}$ & Vector between two line-segment start points, $\mathbf{b}_1-\mathbf{a}_1$ & Ch.~5; App.~B \\
